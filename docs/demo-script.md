# Fresh Fire Studio demo script

## 60-second opening

"Wildfire risk is usually shown as a map. We are making it something you can walk through."

Open the landing page. Pause on the six benchmark fires. Explain:

- public Canadian events;
- real weather, fuel and terrain;
- WS4/FBP fire behaviour;
- Nemora synthetic individual trees;
- a deterministic, scrubbable 3D fire.

## 3-minute walkthrough

1. **Dogrib 2001** — the standard Canadian FBP benchmark. Show the 3D front, tree states, and post-fire scar.
2. **Hay River 2023** — a community evacuation fire. Same model, different landscape.
3. **2003_663** — a large free-burn landscape. Show the scale; note the residual overprediction honestly.
4. Open **Methods**. Say what is real, what is synthetic, and what is not claimed.

## Agency framing

- BC: stand-level fuel treatment, community forest planning, evacuation-risk communication.
- CFS: a public benchmark harness for fire-behaviour comparison, not a replacement for operational prediction.
- UBC FRESH: open code, public data, reproducible evidence.

## Do not say

- "This predicts the next fire."
- "The 3D trees are a measured inventory."
- "The model is calibrated for BC."
- "The small-fire perimeters validate ROS."

## Close

"We can stand up a private instance on your VRI, terrain, fuel and weather, or run a joint benchmark on a fire you already know. The public studio is the front door."
