# FRESH Fire Studio — 90-second reel

## 0:00–0:10 · The problem

**Visual:** a static fire perimeter map. Slow zoom.

**Voice:** “Wildfire risk is usually a map. A line on a page. We made it something you can walk through.”

## 0:10–0:25 · The observed fire

**Visual:** `Dogrib 2001` studio, autoplay. Orbit slightly. Show the observed extent, then zoom until the near-field LOD resolves into individual stems.

**Voice:** “This is a public benchmark fire. Real terrain, real weather, and a synthetic tree population you can inspect. The overview is a canopy impostor; up close, the near field resolves to 500 stems per hectare. The fire moves tree to tree.”

## 0:25–0:40 · The model

**Visual:** `2003_526`; chart at top. Let the observed line and model line separate. Click **Model extent**.

**Voice:** “The model gets the early growth. Then it keeps filling connected fuel. We show that instead of hiding it. This is where the model diverges, and why containment is the next scientific problem.”

## 0:40–0:55 · The evidence

**Visual:** methods page; scroll to validation ladder and the uncomfortable results.

**Voice:** “We benchmark against published envelopes, not one fire. WildfireAV and FARSITE sit near 0.18 Jaccard. Our own reanalysis set is in that range. We report the failures.”

## 0:55–1:10 · The pilot

**Visual:** `/pilot` page, then a landscape image / VRI.

**Voice:** “Bring us one landscape or one fire. We’ll build the 3D case, the observed-versus-model comparison, and a model-limits memo with your team.”

## 1:10–1:20 · The ask

**Visual:** FRESH Lab wordmark, contact.

**Voice:** “Three weeks. One technical contact. A defensible proof. Then we decide what deserves to scale.”

## 1:20–1:30 · Close

**Visual:** studio hero, URL.

**Voice:** “FRESH Fire Studio. UBC Forestry and Environmental Stewardship. See the fire. See the limits. Then decide.”
