Fire behaviour, with the failures visible.

FRESH Fire Studio is a public benchmark harness for Canadian FBP fire behaviour. A CFS pilot applies the validation ladder to one event or a small benchmark set, with observed and free-burn extent shown separately.

The ask

Bring one event or benchmark set and a technical contact. We will run the full ladder, report where the model diverges, and write a model-limits memo suitable for internal or public release.

What you get

Event evidence
  • perimeter and area metrics
  • observed daily progression
  • free-burn extent and divergence day
Technical report
  • validation tier results
  • input and provenance record
  • failure modes and next tests

What we need

  • event perimeter(s), ideally daily
  • hourly weather or station/reanalysis source
  • fuel and terrain, or agreement to use public layers
  • ignition and containment notes if available

Why it is credible

We report published envelopes, not a single pass mark: WildfireAV/ELMFIRE and FARSITE mean Jaccard around 0.18; Prometheus Dogrib 0.21–0.568; FSim BP capture and skill; FOFEM tree mortality. Our own six-case reanalysis benchmark reports median IoU 0.096 and area bias +6.7× to +11.6×. We do not hide that.

What it is not

Not an operational prediction service and not a single-fire calibration exercise. A no-go tier is a valid result.
