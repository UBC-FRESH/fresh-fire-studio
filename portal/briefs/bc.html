Wildfire risk you can see.
One BC landscape. One credible pilot.

FRESH Fire Studio turns public fire data, forest inventory and weather into a runnable 3D fire case. A BC pilot connects that capability to a real community-forest or fuel-treatment decision.

The ask

Bring one community forest or management landscape and the decision you need to support. We will build the case with your technical contact and return a defensible briefing package.

What you get

3D case study
  • observed-extent fire replay
  • tree-level burning and post-fire states
  • treatment / firebreak scenarios
Decision brief
  • risk and treatment interpretation
  • observed vs free-burn comparison
  • uncertainty and model limits

What we need

  • AOI, VRI/stand inventory, terrain and fuel layers
  • weather station or agreed reanalysis source
  • treatment or firebreak polygons
  • a named technical contact

Timeline

Three weeks from scoping to briefing: week 1 ingest, week 2 model runs and comparison, week 3 briefing and release decision.

What it is not

Not an operational prediction service. No tuning to one fire, no hidden validation tier, no private data in a public demo without written approval.
