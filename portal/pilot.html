What we need from you

Minimum

  • AOI or event boundary
  • observed perimeter(s), or a public benchmark event
  • hourly weather, or a station/reanalysis source
  • fuel and terrain, or agreement to use public layers
  • a named technical contact

Ideal

  • VRI or stand inventory
  • treatment / firebreak polygons
  • suppression or containment notes
  • local fire-behaviour observations
  • data-sharing and release decision

What a pilot is not

Not an operational prediction service. We will not tune the model to one fire, hide a failed validation tier, or put private data in a public demo without written approval. The pilot is a joint technical proof, not a product sale.

FRESH Lab at UBC, led by Dr. Gregory Paradis in the Faculty of Forestry & Environmental Stewardship.
