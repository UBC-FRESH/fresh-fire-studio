What you are seeing

This is mission geometry and timing from a fixed 2003_526 scenario. The realistic mode shows 21 CL-415-style drops across three independent aircraft cycles, each returning to the lake to scoop. The selected head segment is about 5.3 km long. It does not show a validated fire effect or containment.

Limits

  • 100 m fire grid cannot resolve a 50–100 m drop footprint;
  • Wheatley evidence is from low-intensity surface fires;
  • the wetness/ROS mapping is diagnostic;
  • no field validation for 2003_526 or BC;
  • the realistic mission leaves open flanks.
